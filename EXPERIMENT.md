# Evaluating Continuum architecture intent

The canonical experiment lives under [experiments/architecture-intent/](experiments/architecture-intent/README.md).

**Question:** Can a focused orchestrator–author dialogue produce a Continuum architecture that an independent human or AI can understand and evolve as well as or better than the original exhaustive package, with less effort?

**Control:** original frozen Recall completeness-oriented authoring package.
**Treatment:** new two-agent intent-oriented authoring package, isolated from the control.

**Primary evaluation:** intent fidelity, independent comprehension, invariant preservation, evolution reasoning and cross-artifact coherence.

**Artifact evaluation:** separate scores for ADL, BDL, technology bindings, decisions and principles, using evidence from the model.

**Process evaluation:** actual elapsed time where available, substantive clarification questions, avoidable questions, revisions, tool friction and information economy.

Do not reward artifact count, line count or mechanical compilation as architectural quality. Do not use the control package to guide treatment authoring. See the [runbook](experiments/architecture-intent/RUNBOOK.md), [rubric](experiments/architecture-intent/QUALITY-RUBRIC.md) and [comparison protocol](experiments/architecture-intent/COMPARISON-PROTOCOL.md).
